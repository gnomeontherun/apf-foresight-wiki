APF 4-Point Peer Review Rubric

All submitted peer evaluations must audit entries across these four criteria:

1. Historical & Epistemic Lineage

Verify that theoretical originators, landmark founding years, institutional nodes, and historical shifts are accurate and non-hallucinated.

2. Methodological Rigor

Ensure facilitation steps, workshop durations, participant scale, and failure modes reflect actionable, professional foresight practice.

3. Primary Literature Verification

Audit and replace any formulaic citations with genuine canonical books, journal papers, and reports with valid DOIs/ISBNs.

4. Pluriversal & Bias Audit

Examine whether entries acknowledge non-Western epistemologies, cognitive biases, decolonial critiques, and systemic limitations.
