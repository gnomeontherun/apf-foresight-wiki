Executive Summary & Definition

Speculative Everything (Dunne & Raby Philosophy) is an authoritative subject within Design Futures, Speculative Fabulation & Diegetics and forms a vital component of the global canon stewarded by the Association of Professional Futurists (APF). Classified under the APF Core Competency: Designing, this entry provides strategic practitioners, institutional leaders, and policy analysts with an actionable, theoretically grounded understanding of its mechanisms and real-world utility.

A design philosophy that uses speculative design and critical prototypes not to solve present commercial problems, but to open up debate and question the future.

Origins & Theoretical Lineage

Articulated by British design theorists Anthony Dunne and Fiona Raby in their landmark 2013 monograph Speculative Everything: Design, Fiction, and Social Dreaming. Building on their work heading the Design Interactions program at the Royal College of Art, Dunne and Raby positioned design as a catalytic medium for public debate, creating conceptual artifacts that challenge consumerist norms and embody alternative societal values.

Step-by-Step Facilitation & Execution Architecture

Executing Speculative Everything (Dunne & Raby Philosophy) requires a disciplined, multistage facilitation process to ensure analytical rigor and deep stakeholder engagement:
  1. Step 1: Problem-Finding vs. Problem-Solving: Shifting design from creating market-ready commodities to formulating provocative questions about technological ethics.
  2. Step 2: Design as Critique: Utilizing physical artifacts and fictional consumer products to expose hidden political, economic, and ideological assumptions.
  3. Step 3: The Plausible/Preferable Cone: Positioning design concepts across cones of possibility (PTP: Probable, Plausible, Possible, Preferable).
  4. Step 4: Public Deliberation Catalyst: Exhibiting speculative design objects in public galleries and civic spaces to stimulate democratic discourse.

Strategic, Policy & Organizational Applications

In applied organizational strategy, corporate innovation, and anticipatory governance, Speculative Everything (Dunne & Raby Philosophy) is routinely utilized to:
  • Technology Ethics: Provoking C-suites and ethicists to evaluate the societal implications of emerging bio-engineering and AI technologies.
  • Curatorial & Museum Exhibitions: Creating immersive museum installations that invite citizens to touch and evaluate alternative futures.
  • Speculative Corporate Foresight: Helping corporate R&D teams explore counter-intuitive applications of emerging patents.
  • Policy Debates: Using speculative physical artifacts to make abstract legislative issues tangible to parliamentarians.

Methodological Critique, Cognitive Biases & Failure Modes

While potent, the effective deployment of Speculative Everything (Dunne & Raby Philosophy) requires vigilance regarding several critical failure modes and cognitive traps:
  • Gallery Enclosure Trap: Risk that speculative design remains an elite art-gallery discourse disconnected from real-world manufacturing and public policy.
  • Eurocentric Privilege: Early speculative design was heavily criticized for reflecting affluent white European anxieties while ignoring global South realities.
  • Functional Ambiguity: Business executives frequently struggle to translate conceptual critical design objects into actionable commercial roadmaps.
